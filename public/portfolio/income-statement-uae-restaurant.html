Executive Summary

Engagement Overview & Outlook
Engagement Summary
Full-cycle financial management for a multi-location restaurant group in the UAE. Monthly P&L and balance sheet within 5 business days of month-end across 9 revenue channels. Automated COGS tracking across 200+ ingredients. Reconciled AED 1.8M+ in aggregator payouts across 5 platforms. Built rolling 13-week cash flow forecasts.
Outlook
The business is marginally profitable with AED 9.8K net income YTD. Strong gross margins (62.3%) provide a solid foundation. Primary focus areas: staff cost efficiency (38.7% of revenue), G&A cost rationalisation (18.2%), and continued aggregator commission management.
Strengths
  • Healthy 62.3% gross margin within industry benchmarks
  • Diversified revenue across 9 channels (dine-in, 5 aggregators, catering)
  • EBITDA positive for 5 of 6 months YTD
  • Food cost tightly managed at 28.4% of revenue
Areas for Improvement
  • Staff costs at 38.7% exceed the 25-35% F&B benchmark
  • G&A at 18.2% needs reduction toward 10-15% target
  • Net margin of 0.8% leaves minimal buffer for contingencies
  • Aggregator commissions at AED 124K average 23.9% — channel cost optimisation needed

Key Achievements

1 Reduced monthly close cycle from 12 to 4 days through automated data ingestion across 9 revenue channels
2 Reconciled AED 1.8M+ in aggregator payouts (Talabat, Noon, Deliveroo, Zomato, Keeta) with 99.8% accuracy
3 Maintained 62.3% gross margin despite volatile commodity pricing

05 — Key Takeaways
Three Core Findings
1. Carrier Concentration: UPS (Group B accounts) alone is 46.39% of total shipping cost. Combined with USPS (31.63%), two carriers account for 78% of spend. Negotiate volume discounts or explore multi-carrier routing to reduce dependency.
2. Fulfillment Dominance: Standard fulfillment operations drive 94.28% of carrier fees. POD is a minor cost center (5.72%). Any fulfillment efficiency gains will have outsized impact on total shipping costs.
3. Store Concentration: Health Brand Store ($4,244) and Fashion Brand Store ($3,939) represent 76% of total carrier fees. A small number of high-volume stores drive the majority of costs — target these for rate negotiation and operational efficiency review.

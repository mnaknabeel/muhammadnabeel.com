Executive Summary

Engagement Overview & Outlook
Engagement Summary
Full-cycle financial analysis for a California-based last-mile delivery startup transitioning from QuickBooks Online. Built monthly P&L with YoY comparatives, revenue channel analysis across PayPal/direct/channel sales, expense benchmarking, cash flow analysis, and DCF valuation. Identified $31.5K in expense reductions and a 223.5% net income swing.
Outlook
The business is well-positioned for growth with strong fundamentals, zero debt, and improving cash reserves. Primary focus areas: diversifying revenue beyond PayPal dependency, managing expense discipline, and building formal operating procedures.
Strengths
  • Exceptional 97.4% gross margin — asset-light service model
  • Zero long-term debt — conservative capital structure
  • 14+ months cash runway — strong liquidity position
  • 30.6% net margin exceeds industry benchmarks significantly
Areas for Improvement
  • Revenue concentration: 53.9% through PayPal, 21% from single customer
  • Extreme monthly volatility: $496 to $11,306 range (22x)
  • $21.7K Venmo informal liability — no repayment terms documented
  • Meals & Entertainment at 10.8% of revenue flags audit risk

Key Achievements

1 Delivered a 223.5% profit swing — from a $19.2K loss to $23.7K net income in a single year
2 Reduced operating expenses by 35.1% ($25.8K) while revenue grew 25.9%
3 Grew cash reserves from $457 to $16,541 — a 36x improvement in liquidity
4 Achieved zero long-term debt with only $1,178 in minimal current liabilities
5 Identified $31.5K in YoY expense savings with 9 out of 19 expense categories reduced
